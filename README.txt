Keep an eye on your HPE ProLiant servers right from Homey. Each server becomes a device that shows whether it is powered on, how much power it draws, its inlet and CPU temperatures, fan speed and overall health, and lets you switch its UID locator light on or off.

Flows can power a server on, shut it down gracefully, force it off, warm-reset or cold-boot it, and react the moment its power state or health changes. Works with iLO 5 (ProLiant Gen10/Gen10 Plus) and iLO 6 (Gen11) over the Redfish API. If your iLO still uses its factory self-signed certificate, pairing offers to allow it with one tap.
